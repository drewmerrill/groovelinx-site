The 20-second version

Every band runs on the same mess — a pile of group texts, a Drive folder of phone recordings nobody replays, and a setlist scribbled in Notes.

GrooveLinx is the one place that remembers your band. Record a rehearsal and it turns into a song-by-song review — what’s tight, what’s rough, what’s gig-ready. It tracks readiness on every song, keeps your setlists and charts where the whole band can see them, and it’s live on your phone.

Not another DAW, not another storage bucket — the thing that keeps the band on the same page between rehearsals.
