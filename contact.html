Contact

Say hello.

A real person reads everything. Usually the one who built it.

Email is best: hello@groovelinx.com

Whatever it is — a question, a bug, a story about a night your band locked in, or an honest “this part is broken and here’s how” — you’ll get a reply from a human within a day or two. There is no ticket system. There is no support tier. There’s a musician with an inbox.

If you’re the one who carries your band and you’re wondering whether GrooveLinx is for you, the honest read is Why Bands Break Up — and if it sounds like your Tuesday, apply as a founding band.

— Drew, GrooveLinx. Born in a band that kept making the drive.

The software disappears. The music remains.
