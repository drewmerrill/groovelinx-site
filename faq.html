Honest answers

“My band will never learn a new app.”

They don’t have to. One person (probably you) keeps the thread; everyone else can start from a text-message link that opens the exact song — no account required to look.

“What happens to our stuff if we quit?”

You take it with you. Export everything, free, any time. If your band ends tomorrow, your memories remain yours.

“Is this going to nag us?”

No. GrooveLinx is quiet by default. A notification has to save more attention than it costs, or we don’t send it.

“Does the AI write music?”

No. Nothing here replaces your band. The intelligence remembers, prepares, and points — you play.

“How much does it cost?”

Founding bands: free during the founding period. Honest answer on future pricing: we haven’t set it, and we won’t do per-musician pricing.

Bring your band

The software disappears. The music remains.
