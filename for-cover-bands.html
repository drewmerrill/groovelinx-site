Why cover bands use it

Your repertoire, finally in one place.

Stop hunting through a spreadsheet, a Drive folder, and three group chats. One catalog the whole band shares — searchable, current, stage-ready.

🎵 The whole book

Every song, with key and BPM up front

Active and library status, per-song key, BPM, structure, lyrics, chord charts and references — all band-shared and searchable. Replace five places with one.

📋 Sets in minutes

Reusable setlists per gig

Build once, reuse across gigs, reorder by drag. Tied to the gig on your calendar and versioned, so each weekend's set stands on its own.

📜 Take the request

Stage-ready charts on the stand

Per-song charts with auto-scroll, swipe or keyboard nav between songs, and key + BPM visible without squinting — under stage lights, on a tablet.

📆 One shared calendar

Gigs and rehearsals everyone can see

Two-way Google Calendar sync the whole band subscribes to, with time-aware classification so personal events don't block rehearsal availability.
