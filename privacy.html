Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2026

GrooveLinx ("we," "our," "the app") is a band rehearsal and performance management tool. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Account info: Your name, email address, and Google account ID (when you sign in with Google).
  • Phone number: Only if you explicitly opt in to SMS notifications. Stored in the band's private database, never sold or shared.
  • Band data: Songs, setlists, gig history, calendar events, RSVPs, and other content you and your bandmates create inside the app.
  • Calendar data: If you connect Google Calendar, we read events from the calendars you authorize. We never read calendars you haven't granted access to.
  • Usage analytics: Page visits, feature usage, and errors — used to improve the product. Not personally identifiable.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the GrooveLinx app's features (rehearsal planning, gig coordination, song management).
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (in-app, browser push, or SMS).
  • To sync your band calendar with Google Calendar when you authorize it.
  • To improve the app based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns.

3. SMS notifications

SMS is opt-in only. If you provide your phone number and enable SMS notifications, we will text you about band events (rehearsal reminders, gig schedules, polls, schedule changes). You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS, or by toggling off SMS in your app settings.

Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Message frequency varies based on your band's activity — typically a few messages per week.

For help, reply HELP to any SMS or email drewmerrill@comcast.net.

We never sell, rent, or share your phone number with third parties. Phone numbers are used solely to send you the notifications you've requested via Twilio (our SMS provider).

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share only with the following service providers, strictly to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase — stores your account and band data.
  • Google Calendar API — only when you authorize calendar sync.
  • Twilio — sends SMS messages on our behalf when you opt in.
  • Cloudflare — hosts our backend worker that proxies API requests.
  • Vercel — hosts the GrooveLinx web app.

None of these providers receive your data for their own marketing purposes.

5. Your rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from app settings, or by emailing us.
  • You can opt out of SMS by replying STOP, and disable browser push from your browser's notification settings.
  • You can revoke Google Calendar access from your Google account's third-party-app permissions page.
  • Residents of California (CCPA) and the EU (GDPR) have additional rights to access, correct, and port their data — email us to exercise them.

6. Data retention

We keep your data as long as you have an active account. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except where required to keep certain records for legal or accounting purposes.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Firebase encryption). We do not store passwords — authentication is handled by Google Sign-In.

8. Children

GrooveLinx is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes

We will update this policy if our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email drewmerrill@comcast.net.
