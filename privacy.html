Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

GrooveLinx ("we," "our," "the app") is a band rehearsal and performance management tool. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What we collect

  • Account info: Your name, email address, and Google account ID (when you sign in with Google).
  • Phone number: Only if you explicitly opt in to SMS notifications. Stored in the band's private database, never sold or shared.
  • Band data: Songs, setlists, gig history, calendar events, RSVPs, and other content you and your bandmates create inside the app.
  • Calendar data: If you connect Google Calendar, we read events from the calendars you authorize. We never read calendars you haven't granted access to.
  • Usage analytics: Page visits, feature usage, and errors — used to improve the product. Not personally identifiable.
  • Social-media interactions: If you or an authorized GrooveLinx administrator connects a supported social-media account or Company Page, GrooveLinx may receive permitted public or authorized interactions with that account or Page, such as comments, replies, mentions, reactions, and related interaction metadata. We collect only data permitted by the connected platform, the permissions granted, and the selected integration scope. The LinkedIn pilot is limited to the GrooveLinx Company Page and does not monitor Drew’s personal LinkedIn profile.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the GrooveLinx app's features (rehearsal planning, gig coordination, song management).
  • To send you notifications you've opted into (in-app, browser push, or SMS).
  • To sync your band calendar with Google Calendar when you authorize it.
  • To improve the app based on aggregate, anonymized usage patterns.
  • Social Inbox review: We may temporarily store and organize permitted social-media interactions so authorized GrooveLinx administrators can review them in a centralized approval queue. Any automated or AI-assisted processing is subject to the connected platform’s rules, the approved integration scope, and applicable data-retention limits.
  • For LinkedIn data, the initial Social Inbox pilot uses no third-party AI processing. If a later LinkedIn configuration enables AI-assisted processing, it will be limited to uses LinkedIn expressly permits, such as prioritization, sentiment analysis, categorization, or similar allowed review functions. GrooveLinx will not use third-party LinkedIn member or Page content to generate or modify a proposed LinkedIn comment, reply, or message unless LinkedIn expressly permits that use and required contractual protections for any third-party processor have been verified.
  • An authorized administrator must review and approve any proposed external action separately. GrooveLinx does not independently publish, reply, react, moderate, delete, or otherwise act externally on a social platform from the observation-only integration. The pilot does not request personal-profile monitoring or write-capable permissions.

3. SMS notifications

SMS is opt-in only. If you provide your phone number and enable SMS notifications, we will text you about band events (rehearsal reminders, gig schedules, polls, schedule changes). You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any SMS, or by toggling off SMS in your app settings.

Standard message and data rates from your carrier may apply. Message frequency varies based on your band's activity — typically a few messages per week.

For help, reply HELP to any SMS or email drewmerrill1029@gmail.com.

We never sell, rent, or share your phone number with third parties. Phone numbers are used solely to send you the notifications you've requested via Twilio (our SMS provider).

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We share only with the following service providers, strictly to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase — stores your account and band data.
  • Google Calendar API — only when you authorize calendar sync.
  • Twilio — sends SMS messages on our behalf when you opt in.
  • Cloudflare — hosts our backend worker that proxies API requests.
  • Vercel — hosts the GrooveLinx web app.
  • Connected social platforms — when an authorized administrator connects a supported social-media account or Company Page, GrooveLinx exchanges permitted interaction data with that platform to retrieve authorized data and display it in the Social Inbox.

None of these providers receive your data for their own marketing purposes. GrooveLinx does not sell social-media interaction data or use it for unrelated advertising. GrooveLinx will not send LinkedIn data to a third-party AI provider for AI processing during the initial pilot. Before any later AI processor is enabled, we will verify the required contractual protections and document the provider and processing purpose.

4a. Google user data

GrooveLinx's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, when you connect Google Calendar:

  • We read events from the calendars you authorize, solely to show your band when members are free or busy and to display shared band events inside GrooveLinx.
  • We create and update events only on your band's shared calendar (rehearsals, gigs, band meetings) when you or a bandmate schedules them in GrooveLinx.
  • We do not use Google Calendar data for advertising, do not sell it, and do not transfer it to third parties except as needed to provide these features or as required by law.
  • Humans do not read your calendar data except with your permission for support, for security purposes, or where required by law.

5. Your rights

  • You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from app settings, or by emailing us.
  • You can opt out of SMS by replying STOP, and disable browser push from your browser's notification settings.
  • You can revoke Google Calendar access from your Google account's third-party-app permissions page.
  • Residents of California (CCPA) and the EU (GDPR) have additional rights to access, correct, and port their data — email us to exercise them.

6. Data retention

We keep your GrooveLinx account and band data as long as you have an active account. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except where required to keep certain records for legal or accounting purposes.

For the LinkedIn pilot, GrooveLinx enforces the applicable LinkedIn storage limits. Member social activity, including comments, mentions, likes, and related metadata, is retained for no longer than 48 hours. Most profile information about non-authenticated members is retained for no longer than 24 hours. If a different LinkedIn rule applies to organization-authored activity or a particular data field, we apply the shorter or otherwise applicable platform limit.

At the end of the applicable retention period, we delete the affected raw payload, normalized interaction, author/profile fields, and associated temporary draft data from Social Inbox storage. Audit records retain only the minimum non-content event needed to demonstrate deletion and system operation, and do not preserve expired LinkedIn content or profile data.

7. Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Firebase encryption). We do not store passwords — authentication is handled by Google Sign-In.

8. Children

GrooveLinx is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

9. Changes

We will update this policy if our practices change. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email drewmerrill1029@gmail.com.
