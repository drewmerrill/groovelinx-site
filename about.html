About

Built by a band that kept making the drive.

Every week, for years, five of us drove across Atlanta to rehearse. Work, kids, traffic — didn’t matter. Tuesday was Tuesday.

Here’s what nobody tells you about being in a band as a grown-up: the music is the easy part. It’s everything around it that wears you down. We’d spend half the night remembering things we’d already decided. The ending we fixed. The key we changed. It was always in the group chat — somewhere.

And still — some nights, it clicked. Five people, one thing. Three and a half minutes where nothing else existed. That’s why we kept making the drive.

We built GrooveLinx because we wanted more of those nights and less of everything else.

What we believe

Bands don’t live inside the musicians. They live in the space between them — between rehearsals, between people, between parts. That space is where bands thrive or die, and it’s what we build for.

Bands don’t have talent problems. They have forgetting problems. So GrooveLinx is, first and always, your band’s memory — everything you decide, learn, and capture, kept.

The honest note lands soft when it has a place to live. Trust isn’t the opposite of accountability; it’s what makes accountability survivable.

Practice happens at home. Rehearsal happens together. The room is too scarce to spend on what could’ve been done alone.

And the software should disappear. If you notice GrooveLinx during the music, we built it wrong.

What we refuse

We will never replace your band. We will never make music feel like homework. We will never judge a musician. We will never sell your band’s data, show you an ad, or make leaving hard.

Your band’s memory belongs to your band — exportable, always. If your band ends tomorrow, your memories remain yours.

We are not building software. We are protecting the moments that make people want to stay in bands. If that sounds like something your band needs, bring your band.

The software disappears. The music remains.
