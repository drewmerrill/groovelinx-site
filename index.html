Founding Bands

We’re not launching. We’re onboarding a small number of founding bands — and working with each one directly.

Here’s the honest picture: GrooveLinx has been built and battle-tested by one working band for over a year — ours. Every feature exists because a real rehearsal or a real gig demanded it. Now it needs bands that aren’t ours. Bands that will use it on their own Tuesdays and tell us the truth.

What founding bands get: the product, free, for the founding period · a direct line to the people building it · a real say in what it becomes · your band’s memory, compounding from day one — and yours forever, exportable, whatever happens.

What we ask: use it for real. Tell us the truth, especially when it’s unflattering. That’s the whole deal.
