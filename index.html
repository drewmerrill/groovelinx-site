No setup required. No catalog to build. No learning curve.

2. Setlist + Rehearsal

One tap to start playing

A starter setlist is auto-created. Hit Start Rehearsal. GrooveLinx tracks timing, songs, and flow. You just play.

What happened. What changed. What's next.
