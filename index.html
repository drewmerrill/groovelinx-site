01
Plan

Songs, setlists, and schedules — in one place the whole band can see.

Stop hunting through Slack threads, group chats, and shared docs. Plan once, in a place built for it.

One source of truth for the whole band

Active songs, library songs, structure, key, BPM, references — all band-shared, all searchable, all current. Replace your spreadsheet, your Drive folder, and your "where's the chart?" Slack thread with one place.

🎵 Songs

Your band's catalog — structured, searchable, shared

Active vs library status. Per-song key, BPM, structure, lyrics, chord charts, and reference recordings. One source of truth instead of five.

📋 Setlists

Build, share, and reuse setlists across gigs

Drag-and-drop reorder. Tied to specific gigs in your calendar. Versioned so last weekend's set doesn't overwrite next weekend's.

📆 Shared Google Calendar mode

One calendar, syncing both ways

Sync rehearsals and gigs to a Google Calendar everyone in the band can subscribe to. Pick which calendars to import. Time-aware classification keeps personal events from blocking rehearsal availability.

🗓 Gigs queue

What's next, and what to prepare for

Upcoming gigs surface what to rehearse, what setlist needs building, and which songs aren't ready yet. The calendar drives the work.

02
Rehearse

Walk into rehearsal knowing what to work on.

No more "what should we play tonight?" Rehearsal becomes intentional — with a plan that adapts to what your band actually needs.

Walkthroughs that capture what happened

Run a song, take notes mid-walkthrough, jump sections, log readiness per member. Every rehearsal feeds the next one's agenda — and the next one's gig prep.

📝 Rehearsal plans

An agenda built from your band's data

Suggests which songs to walk through based on readiness, recent rehearsal coverage, and what's coming up on the gig calendar. Edit it, ignore it, or hand it to the band.

📊 Readiness

Per-song, per-member readiness

See which songs are stage-ready, which are still woodshedding, and which haven't been touched in a month. Weak links surface automatically.

▶️ Walkthrough mode

Run through a song with notes flowing

Timed walkthroughs capture what was rehearsed and how it went. Take notes mid-song. Stop, restart, jump sections.

🎯 Practice actions

One specific thing to work on next

After every rehearsal: "Focus on the bridge of Estimated Prophet" — not generic encouragement. Action-sized, song-specific.

04
Review

Learn parts faster — without burning hours on YouTube.

Tools that speed the part-learning process. Honest framing: drafts you clean up, not magic transcription.

Stems mixer + Harmony Lab in one app

Pull a recording in, separate drums/bass/vocals/keys/guitar, mute and solo per part. Split lead vs. backing vocals, pan each into its own ear, loop the bars you're learning. Auto-draft notation appears alongside — review and clean up by hand.

🎚 Stems lens

Isolate drums, bass, vocals, keys, guitar — from your recordings

Self-hosted Demucs separation gives you 4 or 6 stems per song. Mute, solo, volume, pan, and tempo controls let you practice your part with the rest of the band still in the mix — or by itself.

🎤 Harmony Lab

Split lead vs. backing vocals. Loop a phrase. Sing along.

LALAL.AI separates a recording into lead and backing tracks, lets you pan each into its own ear, and loop the bars you're learning. Auto-draft notation appears alongside — review and clean up by hand.

We don't claim perfect harmony separation or perfect transcription. We claim faster part-learning than YouTube + paper.

🎼 BPM & key auto-detect

Tempo and key, populated from the audio

Drop a reference recording in — BPM and key get detected and saved to the song. Metronome auto-tunes. Manual override always available.

🧠 Song intelligence

What we know about your songs grows over time

Sections, chords, lyrics, structure, tempo — populated as you use the app, not as a one-shot import. The catalog gets sharper rehearsal by rehearsal.

05
Improve

What changed. What's working. What's next.

Every rehearsal teaches the next one. Every gig feeds the dashboard. The band gets sharper over time — on purpose.

"What's happening with my band?" — one screen, every morning

Open the app and see the pulse: what changed across songs, takes, calendar, polls, ideas, gig prep. Cross-session insights show what tightened, what slipped, and what to do next. Real data, not "good job."

📊 Post-rehearsal insights

"This rehearsal vs last rehearsal" — for each song

Cross-session attribution. Which songs improved. Which transitions tightened. Which sections need work. Real data, not "good job."

📰 Activity feed

Everything that changed, in one feed

The Home feed shows what changed across the band: new takes, status changes, calendar events, polls, ideas, gig prep. Open the app, see the pulse.

💓 Band pulse

Readiness across songs, members, and weeks

A glanceable view of how prepared the band is for what's next. Not a vanity metric — a way to spot the song that's quietly slipping before the gig.

🔔 Notifications

Three layers, one band

In-app banners for active users, browser/OS push for backgrounded tabs, SMS for the bandmate who never opens the app. Keep everyone aligned without anyone getting buried.

FAQ

Questions bands ask.

What is GrooveLinx?

GrooveLinx is the operating system for working bands: one web app to practice, rehearse, play, review, and improve, built around your songs, setlists, and shared schedule.

Is GrooveLinx a setlist app?

It includes a full setlist manager — build once, reuse across gigs, and print stage-ready setlists — but it’s broader: setlists are one of five connected systems.

How does GrooveLinx help with rehearsals?

It builds a rehearsal plan from your band’s data, tracks per-song and per-member readiness, and points you at the one thing to work on next.

How is it different from a group chat with a shared calendar?

Group chats lose context every week. GrooveLinx keeps your band’s operational memory — songs, readiness, setlists, and history — in one place that carries over.

Can the whole band use it together?

Yes — songs, setlists, schedule, and readiness are shared, so everyone sees one source of truth, with RSVP so you know your lineup.

What kinds of bands is it for?

Working bands that rehearse and perform — jam bands, cover bands, tributes, and church or worship teams.

What does it cost?

GrooveLinx is in invite-only beta. Join the list for the next bands we onboard.

Do I need to install anything?

No — it runs in the browser and installs as a phone or tablet app (PWA); no app store needed.
